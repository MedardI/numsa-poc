We Can Represent You!

Some industries we represent

NUMSA is the largest metalworkers’ union in South Africa, but our representation goes far beyond metal. We unite workers across diverse industries to ensure their voices are heard, their rights protected, and their working conditions improved.

View all our industries
01

Motor Industry

NUMSA represents workers in dealerships, workshops, panel beating and tyre fitment. We make sure your skills are respected, your wages are fair and your rights are not ignored.

02

Metal and Engineering

This is where NUMSA’s roots are. We organise workers in steel, foundries, fabrication and engineering shops. We fight for better wages, safer conditions and strong agreements that protect jobs.

03

Automotive Manufacturing

We protect workers in the car factories and component plants. We fight for decent pay, safe conditions and secure jobs. When bosses push outsourcing or retrenchments, NUMSA stands firm to defend the workers who keep this industry alive.

04

Electrical Engineering

NUMSA organises workers in electrical manufacturing and engineering. We use our collective power to win decent wages and defend rights in this critical industry.

05

Tyre and Rubber

NUMSA represents workers in dealerships, workshops, panel beating and tyre fitment. We make sure your skills are respected, your wages are fair and your rights are not ignored.

06

Aerospace

NUMSA represents highly skilled aerospace workers. We fight retrenchments and demand respect for the skills that are vital for South Africa’s development.

07

Telecoms and Electronics

We unite workers in electronics and telecoms manufacturing. NUMSA resists casual contracts and agency exploitation so that workers have secure jobs and fair treatment.

08

Renewable Energy

As South Africa moves to renewable energy, NUMSA ensures the transition creates decent, secure jobs. We organise solar and wind workers so that green energy is also workers’ energy.
